Atlas · Explore Earth with AI（和AI一起探索地球）

Atlas drops you at a random Google Street View location anywhere in the world and writes a short letter about what you are looking at: the place, its culture and history, grounded in the frame you are facing. The interface is available in English and Chinese.

Atlas 把你随机放到世界某处的谷歌街景里，并结合你正在看的画面，写一封关于这个地方的地理、文化与历史的短信。界面支持中文和英文。

What you can do

  • Explore: random Street View stops across countries, with Atlas letters, follow-up questions and Atlas Voice conversation.
  • Guess Where: a satellite-image geography game, solo or against an AI opponent.
  • Online duel: 1v1 rounds with room codes or quick matchmaking.
  • Odyssey: let your own AI agent travel through Street View and send you an illustrated letter.
  • Footprints: a shared map of where Atlas has been.

Requires JavaScript. Made by 王雨峰 (Yufeng Wang).
